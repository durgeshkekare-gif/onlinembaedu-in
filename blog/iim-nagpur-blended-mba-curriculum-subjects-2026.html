IIM Nagpur Blended MBA — Curriculum Overview

The IIM Nagpur Blended MBA curriculum is a full 2-year MBA programme equivalent to the residential MBA — designed for working professionals who bring real-world context to every case discussion. The curriculum is divided into 4 semesters (2 per year) with core mandatory courses, functional electives, and integrative modules.

Year 1 — Foundational MBA Modules

Semester 1 (Online Classes + Campus Module 1)

  • Financial Accounting and Reporting
  • Managerial Economics
  • Organisational Behaviour
  • Statistics for Management
  • Marketing Management — Fundamentals
  • Business Communication for Leaders

Semester 2 (Online + Campus Module 2)

  • Corporate Finance
  • Operations Management
  • Strategic Management — Introduction
  • Human Resource Management
  • Business Research Methods
  • Marketing Management — Advanced

Year 2 — Advanced and Elective Modules

Semester 3 (Online + Campus Module 3)

  • Corporate Strategy and Competitive Dynamics
  • Business Analytics and Decision Making
  • International Business
  • Entrepreneurship and Innovation
  • Elective Block 1 (choose from Finance / Marketing / Operations / HR specialisation tracks)

Semester 4 (Online + Campus Module 4 — Graduation)

  • Leadership and Organisational Transformation
  • Digital Business Strategy
  • Corporate Governance and Ethics
  • Capstone Project / Consulting Project
  • Elective Block 2

Frequently Asked Questions

What subjects are taught in IIM Nagpur Blended MBA?

Core subjects across 4 semesters: Financial Accounting, Managerial Economics, Organisational Behaviour, Marketing Management, Corporate Finance, Operations Management, Strategic Management, HRM, Business Analytics, Digital Business Strategy, Leadership, Corporate Governance, and Capstone Project. Electives in Finance, Marketing, Operations, or HR specialisation tracks.

Is the IIM Nagpur Blended MBA curriculum the same as the regular MBA?

The Blended MBA covers the same management disciplines as IIM Nagpur's regular programme — strategic management, finance, marketing, operations, HR, and analytics. The delivery format is adapted (blended) but the academic rigour is equivalent. Graduates receive the same MBA degree as the residential batch.

How many hours per week does the IIM Nagpur MBA require?

Year 1: approximately 12-15 hours per week (live sessions on Wed/Sat evenings + Sunday + self-study). Year 2: approximately 8-12 hours per week. Campus immersion weeks require full-time commitment for 5 days, 4 times per year. Most working professionals manage this alongside full-time employment with advance planning.
