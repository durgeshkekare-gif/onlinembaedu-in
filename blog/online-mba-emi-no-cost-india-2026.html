Online MBA on No-Cost EMI — How It Works

No-cost EMI (zero-interest EMI) means you pay the total programme fee in monthly instalments with zero additional interest. The university or education platform partners with banks/NBFCs who absorb the interest cost. For online MBAs, no-cost EMI makes the ₹1-1.5L total fee payable at ₹5K-8K/month — making it accessible even on lower monthly incomes.

Banks Offering No-Cost EMI for Online MBA

  • HDFC Bank — most widely accepted across universities
  • ICICI Bank — credit card and personal loan EMI options
  • Axis Bank — FlexiPay EMI on credit cards
  • Bajaj Finserv — education EMI, no credit card required
  • KreditBee / NBFC — for candidates without credit cards

Eligibility for No-Cost EMI

For credit card no-cost EMI: credit card with minimum limit of ₹50K-₹1.5L (varies by bank), good credit score (720+), card not blocked for merchant category. For NBFC/loan EMI (Bajaj Finserv, KreditBee): income proof, PAN card, bank statement, credit score check.

IIM Programme EMI — What to Know

IIM programmes also have EMI options at higher amounts: IIM Nagpur Blended MBA (~₹50K/month over 24 months), IIM Ahmedabad AGMP (~₹67K/month), IIM Kozhikode NAMP (~₹40K/month), IIM Trichy SMP (~₹30K/month). Education loans (SBI, HDFC Credila) are also available for IIM programmes — often at 9-11% interest, with deferred repayment starting after programme completion.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which online MBA has the lowest monthly EMI in India 2026?

Symbiosis Online MBA (₹1.07L total) has the lowest monthly EMI — approximately ₹4,458/month on 24-month no-cost EMI. LPU Online MBA (₹1.20L) is second lowest at ~₹5,000/month on 24 months. Manipal MUJ, Amrita, and Amity (₹1.35L each) work out to ~₹5,625/month on 24-month EMI.

Is no-cost EMI really zero interest for online MBA?

Yes — no-cost EMI is genuinely zero interest for you. The interest is paid by the university/education platform to the bank as a merchant subsidy. You pay exactly the programme fee divided by number of months. There are no hidden charges unless you miss a payment, which attracts penalty.

Can I get an education loan for online MBA in India?

Yes — SBI Student Loan, HDFC Credila, Axis Bank Education Loan and Avanse Financial Services all offer education loans for online MBA from UGC-DEB approved universities. Loan amounts up to ₹10-20L. Repayment typically starts 6-12 months after course completion. Compare interest rates — typically 9-14% p.a.
