IIM Nagpur Blended MBA — Online or Not?

The IIM Nagpur Blended MBA has significant online components: all regular classes are delivered via live online sessions (Wednesday/Saturday evenings and Sunday mornings). However, it also has mandatory in-person campus immersions at IIM Nagpur 4 times per year. It is a blended programme — not fully online.

Who Needs an IIM MBA vs Online MBA

Choose IIM Nagpur Blended MBA if: You specifically need the IIM MBA credential for career positioning. You have 3+ years experience and the ₹12.10L investment is feasible. You're targeting senior management roles where IIM credentials materially change how you're evaluated.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is IIM Nagpur Blended MBA an online MBA?

Technically, IIM Nagpur Blended MBA is a blended programme — online classes + mandatory campus immersions at IIM Nagpur (4 × 5 days per year). It is not a 100% online programme. Unlike standard online MBAs (Manipal, Amrita, Symbiosis), it requires physical attendance at IIM Nagpur 4 times per year. The trade-off: it gives a full IIM MBA degree vs an online MBA's university degree.

How is IIM Nagpur Blended MBA different from a regular online MBA?

Key differences: IIM Nagpur MBA costs ₹12.10L (vs ₹80K–₹1.5L online MBA). It requires 4 campus visits per year (vs 0 for online MBA). It gives IIM institutional credentials and alumni status (vs university degree from online MBA). It requires 3 years work experience and an entrance test (vs no work exp needed for most online MBAs). The IIM credential carries categorically higher career weight.

Which is better — IIM Nagpur Blended MBA or Manipal Online MBA?

Manipal Online MBA (₹1.35L, 2 years, NAAC A+) is the right choice if budget is the primary constraint and you need a valid MBA degree at minimal cost. IIM Nagpur Blended MBA (₹12.10L, 2 years, AACSB) is right if you want IIM institutional credentials, alumni network, and are willing to invest ₹12.10L for the career positioning that comes with an IIM MBA degree.
